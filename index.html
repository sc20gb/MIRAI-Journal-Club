Medical Imaging and Gene Rative AI

MIRAI Lab is a research group at the University of Leeds working at the intersection of medical imaging, computer vision, generative AI, vision-language models, and intelligent healthcare technologies. Our research aims to develop robust, interpretable, and clinically meaningful AI systems for medical image understanding, surgical video analysis, medical robotics, and computer-assisted healthcare.

Our group brings together researchers working on surgical tool pose estimation, digital histopathology and molecular spectroscopy, virtual patients for medical training, surgical video understanding and anticipation, and medical vision-language models. Through interdisciplinary collaboration, we seek to build AI methods that connect visual perception, multimodal reasoning, and real-world clinical needs.
